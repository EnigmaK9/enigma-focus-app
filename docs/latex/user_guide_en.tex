\section{Introduction}
\textbf{Enigma Focus} is a production-grade digital wellbeing and productivity app for Android engineered around the Unix philosophy: \textit{do one thing and do it exceptionally well}. It empowers you to reclaim your time and focus by eliminating compulsive dopaminergic phone habits through hardware-level GPU monochrome mode, mindful breathing friction, and aggressive sleep schedule lockouts.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=codeBg,colframe=accentGreen,title=\textbf{Key Feature Highlights}]
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Sub-20ms Accessibility Interception}: Real-time app and website blocking with zero battery drain.
    \item \textbf{10-Second Mindful Breathing Challenge}: Inhale, Hold, and Exhale guide before unlocking.
    \item \textbf{Hardware GPU Monochrome}: Direct Daltonizer display compositor control (\texttt{WRITE\_SECURE\_SETTINGS}).
    \item \textbf{Aggressive Bedtime Lockout (10:30 PM -- 6:30 AM)}: Strict 1-minute emergency cap during sleep hours.
    \item \textbf{Unix Philosophy \& CLI Automation}: 14 Broadcast Intents for ADB, Termux, and Tasker integration.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

\section{Initial Setup \& Required Permissions}

To deliver unbreakable focus protection, Enigma Focus utilizes two core Android system permissions:

\subsection{1. Accessibility Service}
Allows the engine to instantly detect when a restricted app or distracting web domain is opened:
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item Go to \textbf{Settings} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Accessibility} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Downloaded Apps / Installed Services}.
    \item Tap \textbf{Enigma Focus} and enable the toggle.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{2. Hardware Grayscale Permission (WRITE\_SECURE\_SETTINGS)}
Enables the app to command the GPU display compositor to enter monochrome mode. Connect your phone to a computer with USB Debugging enabled and run:
\begin{lstlisting}[language=bash]
adb shell pm grant com.example.enigmafocus android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Core Features \& Daily Operations}

\subsection{Manual Focus Timer Sessions}
On the main \textbf{Focus} tab:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Select your desired focus session chip: \textbf{25 min} (Pomodoro), \textbf{1 hour}, \textbf{4 hours}, or \textbf{9h Workday}.
    \item Tap \textbf{Start Session}. Hardware grayscale turns on (if enabled), an ongoing status bar countdown appears, and all target apps are blocked.
    \item To conclude early, tap \textbf{Stop Session}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Scheduled Recurring Focus Intervals}
Enigma Focus includes two preconfigured default intervals:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Workday Shift}: \texttt{07:30 - 16:30} (Monday to Friday).
    \item \textbf{Rest / Sleep}: \texttt{22:30 - 06:30} (Every day).
\end{enumerate}
Tap the \textbf{Edit} button on any interval to adjust start/end times and active days, or tap \textbf{+ Add Schedule / Interval} to create custom routines (e.g. Evening Study from 18:00 to 20:30).

\subsection{Mindful Breathing Challenge}
When you launch a blocked app (e.g. Instagram, TikTok, or Reddit), an immersive dark screen immediately covers the display:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Inhale} deeply through your nose (Seconds 10 to 8).
    \item \textbf{Hold} your breath calmly (Seconds 7 to 5).
    \item \textbf{Exhale} slowly and release tension (Seconds 4 to 1).
    \item Once completed, choose \textbf{"Return to Focus"} to go Home or \textbf{"Use app for 1 minute"} for emergency access.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Aggressive Bedtime Lockout}
During your sleep window (\texttt{22:30 -- 06:30}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item Every screen turn-on or phone unlock is immediately intercepted by the bedtime lock overlay.
    \item Emergency access is capped at \textbf{strictly 60 seconds} after passing the 10s breathing challenge.
    \item When 60s expire or the screen is turned off, the background watchdog immediately re-locks the device.
    \item Emergency phone calls (\texttt{InCallUI}) and alarm clocks (\texttt{DeskClock}) remain permanently accessible.
\end{itemize}

\section{Managing Blocked Applications}
On the \textbf{Apps} tab:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Use the real-time search bar to find any application installed on your device.
    \item Toggle individual switches to add or remove apps from your blocklist.
    \item Tap \textbf{Block Popular} to instantly restrict major social media and distraction platforms in 1 tap.
\end{itemize}

\section{Unix CLI Broadcasts \& Script Automation}
Control Enigma Focus directly from terminal, Termux, or Tasker via standard Android broadcast intents:
\begin{lstlisting}[language=bash]
# Start a 45-minute focus session
adb shell am broadcast -a com.example.enigmafocus.action.START_SESSION --ei duration_minutes 45

# Stop active session
adb shell am broadcast -a com.example.enigmafocus.action.STOP_SESSION

# Toggle hardware grayscale
adb shell am broadcast -a com.example.enigmafocus.action.TOGGLE_GRAYSCALE

# Query status JSON
adb shell am broadcast -a com.example.enigmafocus.action.GET_STATUS

# Apply template (DEFAULT, WORKDAY_ONLY, BEDTIME_STRICT, DIGITAL_DETOX)
adb shell am broadcast -a com.example.enigmafocus.action.APPLY_TEMPLATE --es template BEDTIME_STRICT
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Xiaomi / MIUI / HyperOS Optimization Guide}
To prevent aggressive OEM battery managers from stopping background protection:
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Autostart}: \texttt{Settings $\rightarrow$ Apps $\rightarrow$ Enigma Focus $\rightarrow$ Autostart $\rightarrow$ Allow}.
    \item \textbf{Battery Saver}: Select \texttt{No restrictions}.
    \item \textbf{Display Pop-up Windows}: Set to \texttt{Always Allow}.
\end{enumerate}
